Frequently asked questions

How do I invite friends to a trip?

Open Settings → tap the QR code icon. Share the link or QR code with participants. The link takes them straight into your trip.

Data does not sync between devices

Make sure you are online. The icon in the top right shows the status: green — online, red — offline. While offline, data is saved locally and synced once the connection is back.

I accidentally left a trip. How do I get back?

Ask the organiser to send the invitation link again. Open the link — you will be taken back to the trip and can pick your name.

OCR does not recognise a receipt

Make sure the image is sharp and well lit. The app uses AI (Claude) for recognition, so the result depends on photo quality. You can always enter items manually.

How do I delete a trip?

Only the organiser can delete a trip. Open Settings → scroll down → “Delete trip”. This cannot be undone and removes all data from the database.

The app does not open offline

After the first launch the app is cached for offline use. If that did not happen, open the app online at least once; the browser will then keep it for use without internet.

How do I add the app to my home screen?

Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen”.
iOS (Safari): “Share” button → “Add to Home Screen”.

Is my data safe?

Data is only available to participants who have the invitation link. Every database request is checked against the token. More in our Privacy Policy.
